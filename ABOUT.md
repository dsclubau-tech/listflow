# What is ListFlow?

## The Short Version

ListFlow is a private seller operations dashboard built for eBay sellers who source products from Amazon Australia. It brings together everything a seller needs — importing products, publishing listings, tracking supplier prices, managing live inventory, and researching the market — into one centralized place.

Instead of juggling Amazon, eBay, spreadsheets, and separate tools, a seller opens ListFlow and does it all from there.

---

## The Problem It Solves

Running an eBay dropshipping operation manually is slow, error-prone, and exhausting. Here is what sellers typically have to deal with:

- **Copying product data by hand.** Every time a new product is listed, someone has to manually transfer the title, images, description, price, category, and a long list of required item details from Amazon to eBay. That is tedious and easy to get wrong.

- **eBay rejecting uploads.** eBay has strict rules about what information a listing must include — things like product type, size, brand, or part numbers. If any of those are missing, the upload fails. Sellers often do not find out until they hit "submit."

- **Supplier prices changing without warning.** If Amazon raises the price of a product and the eBay listing is not updated, the seller either loses money on every sale or has to manually check prices constantly.

- **Existing eBay inventory scattered and unmanaged.** Sellers who already have listings on eBay have no easy way to bring that inventory into a central system for ongoing management.

- **Long operations with no visibility.** Checking prices across hundreds of products, importing large batches of listings, or running market research can take a long time. Without a proper system, there is no way to pause, resume, track progress, or recover from errors.

ListFlow was built to solve all of these problems.

---

## What ListFlow Does

### Import Products from Amazon Australia

A seller pastes an Amazon AU product link and ListFlow automatically pulls in everything needed to create an eBay listing — the title, images, description, price, category, variants (like sizes or colors), and all required item details. If a product has both a regular price and a sale or deal price, the seller chooses which one to track.

### Build and Edit Drafts Before Going Live

Imported products start as drafts. The seller can review and edit every detail — rewrite the title, adjust pricing, manage images, tweak the description, and fill in any missing item specifics — before the listing ever touches eBay.

ListFlow validates the draft against eBay requirements before allowing it to go live, so upload failures are caught early rather than after submitting.

### Publish and Revise eBay Listings

When a draft is ready, ListFlow publishes it directly to eBay. For live listings, sellers can update prices, descriptions, titles, fees, policies, and more — either one at a time or in bulk across many products at once.

### Track Supplier Prices Automatically

ListFlow monitors the Amazon AU buy price for every tracked product. When a supplier raises or lowers their price, ListFlow flags it as a pending change. The seller reviews the change and decides whether to apply it (which updates the eBay listing automatically) or dismiss it.

This protects sellers from silently selling products at a loss when Amazon prices go up.

### Import Existing eBay Inventory

Sellers who already have listings on eBay do not have to start from scratch. ListFlow can pull existing active listings directly from eBay and import them into the system, so they can be managed going forward alongside new products.

### Research the eBay Market

Before pricing a product, sellers can use ListFlow research tools to see what other sellers are charging, what is actually selling, and at what price. Research results are saved for 24 hours so the same query does not have to be run repeatedly.

### Manage Products in Bulk

The Products page is the main operational hub for live inventory. Sellers can:

- Search and filter their entire catalog by title, price, profit, status, ad type, and more
- Edit individual products inline without leaving the page
- Select multiple products and apply bulk actions — price updates, holds, resumes, ending listings, or removal
- Export their product data to CSV
- Track which listings are running eBay promoted ads and at what rate

### The Action Center

All long-running operations — price checks, bulk edits, eBay imports, research batches, and listing actions — are tracked in the Action Center. Sellers can monitor progress, pause and resume jobs, and see exactly what succeeded and what failed, with no guessing.

### Settings and Templates

ListFlow lets sellers configure everything once and reuse it:

- Default shipping, returns, and payment policies
- Description templates for product listings
- Keyword blacklists for cleaning up titles and descriptions
- Default item details applied across products
- Profit margin targets and fee structures

## Why "ListFlow"?

The name comes from the core workflow the app is built around: listing products on eBay, made to flow.

Before ListFlow, getting a product from an Amazon AU page to a live eBay listing involved a series of disconnected, manual steps — copy data here, check prices there, fix errors somewhere else. The process had friction at every stage.

ListFlow was designed to make that entire process continuous and smooth — a flow from first import to live listing to ongoing management, all in one place. The name reflects that intention: listing, made to flow.

---

## Who It Is For

ListFlow is built for eBay sellers — specifically those who:

- Source products from Amazon Australia to sell on eBay (a dropshipping model)
- Manage one or more eBay store profiles
- Deal with a catalog large enough that manual management is not practical
- Need reliable automation for pricing, uploads, and inventory management

It is a private operational tool, not a public marketplace or SaaS platform. It is designed to give a seller or a small team complete control over their eBay operation from a single, purpose-built dashboard.

---

## The Big Picture

ListFlow turns what was once a slow, scattered, manual process into a structured, centralized workflow.

A seller day with ListFlow might look like this:

1. Check the Action Center to see if overnight price checks flagged any changes.
2. Review and apply or dismiss the pending price updates.
3. Add a few new products from Amazon AU links.
4. Edit and publish the new drafts to eBay.
5. Run market research on a category they are exploring.
6. Do a bulk edit on a group of listings to update shipping policies.

All of that happens in one app, with full visibility into what is running, what succeeded, and what needs attention.

That is ListFlow.
